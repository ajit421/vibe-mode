Vibe Mode privacy

Updated 25 November 2026

Vibe Mode works without an account. You can use it without giving a name, an email or a password. This page lists everything it keeps, and why.

On your device

Your modes, playlists, pictures and settings are kept in your browser’s storage. They stay on your device unless you use cloud save (below).

Signing in and cloud save

Signing in is optional and uses your Google account. Signed in, your modes, playlists, pictures and settings are saved on our server as you change them, so they are there on every device you sign in on. Without signing in, nothing is saved on our server: everything stays in this browser.

From Google we keep only:

  • Google’s ID number for your account, so the same account always opens the same cloud copy;
  • your email address, name and profile picture, to show which account you are signed in with.

We get nothing else from Google: no contacts, no files, no access to your account. Each signed-in browser holds a long random key; our server keeps only a scrambled form of it.

AI assistants. If you connect Claude, ChatGPT or another assistant to your Vibe Mode, you sign in with Google on our page and it gets its own key for your account. It can then read and change your modes and playlists, and control the player in tabs where you allow it. Account → Sign out other devices disconnects every assistant and other device.

To sign out, use Account → Sign out: your modes stay in that browser. To delete everything we keep about you — the cloud copy, the Google link and your history — use Account → Delete my cloud copy. It cannot be undone. Your modes stay in your browser.

Usage counts

To see how the site is used, each browser tab sends a few counts:

  • a visit, counted by browser, operating system, device type and country, as daily totals;
  • how long the tab was open, on screen and playing music;
  • while music plays, which mode, playlist and song;
  • which features were used (panels opened, skips, shortcuts and so on), as totals.

Not signed in, these counts are anonymous. They carry no name, email, account or IP address. The only id is a random one that lives in that one tab and is gone when you close it, so one visit is never linked to another.

Signed in, your visits, your listening (which songs, modes and playlists, and for how long) and the features you use are saved with your account, and the site’s owner can see them. This is on unless you turn it off. To stop it, switch off Settings → Link my activity to my account, and you’re counted anonymously from then on. Deleting your cloud copy deletes this history too. Linked visits are kept for 90 days. Listening history is kept until you delete your cloud copy.

No IP addresses are stored. If your browser sends Global Privacy Control, it sends none of these counts at all.

We also use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page views without cookies.

Others involved

  • YouTube plays the music, so YouTube’s own privacy policy applies to the player.
  • Cloudflare hosts the site and the database.
  • Google handles Google sign-in, if you use it.

What we don’t do

No advertising. No selling or sharing of data. No tracking across other sites. No IP address logs in our database. To slow down abuse, such as a script creating thousands of fake accounts, the server counts requests per IP address for a few minutes, in memory only. The address is never written down. Your activity is linked to you only through your own Vibe Mode account, never to other sites or services.
